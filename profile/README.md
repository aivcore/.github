# AIVCORE

**Turning Real-World Sensing Into Action.**

AIVCORE focuses on visual perception and spatial intelligence, exploring how images, video, and sensor data can help us better understand the physical world.

Our work brings together computer vision, video analytics, 3D scene understanding, and sensor-based perception, with a focus on practical applications.

### Areas of Interest

- **Visual Intelligence**: Detection, tracking, and understanding events in images and video.
- **Spatial Intelligence**: 3D reconstruction, mapping, and understanding physical environments.
- **Intelligent Systems**: Perception for industrial applications, automation, and robotics.

### What We're Building

We're developing AIVCORE around real-world problems, with an emphasis on practical experimentation, reliable engineering, and adaptable solutions.

This GitHub organization is home to our public technical work, demonstrations, and selected research as they become available.
